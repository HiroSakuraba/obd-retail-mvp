Pick a fault family, tap “Scan vehicle” to inject a VIN, the DTC, and live values; the diagnosis is deterministic — diagnostic_score(h) ∝ prior(h) × evidence multipliers from the evidence graph. Scores are uncalibrated expert weights, not true probabilities: two rules read from one measurement count once (the stronger wins), and a score alone never sells a part — replacement repairs need confirming evidence first.

Design rule: a DTC is evidence, not a parts recommendation. Nothing below names a part until the evidence supports a cause — and even then the basket is provisional until confirmed. Watch P0420: the first test points at the catalytic converter, but the basket stays empty until an independent check rules out an exhaust leak. Watch P0171: the trim test only unlocks guidance ("inspect the intake next") — the hose itself stays gated until a leak is seen. And the graph never names a SKU: the part, price, and fitment come from a separate fitment layer keyed off the repair action and the VIN.
